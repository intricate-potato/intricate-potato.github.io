I am Abdullah (Chowdhury Mohammad Abdullah), a researcher investigating the human impacts of artificial intelligence, particularly language models and recommender systems. I am a Master of Computer Science student at the [Faculty of Computer Science](https://www.dal.ca/faculty/computerscience.html), [Dalhousie University](https://www.dal.ca/), where I work in the Persuasive Computing Lab under the supervision of [Dr. Rita Orji](https://web.cs.dal.ca/~orji/).

My work sits at the intersection of [human-computer interaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human%E2%80%93computer_interaction), [computational social science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_social_science), and the ethical dimensions of computing. As modern AI systems become more complex and socially embedded, it is increasingly clear to me that central challenges in human-AI interaction, safety, and explainability can often be answered through foundational work in the social sciences. My research bridges these fields by translating established paradigms into computational contexts, such as drawing on [sociolinguistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociolinguistics) and experimental [matched-guise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matched-guise_test) techniques to [audit covert model prejudice]({{ '/projects/dialect-bias-llms/' | relative_url }}), adapting structural [narratology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narratology) for [news recommendation]({{ '/projects/narrative-news-recsys/' | relative_url }}), and leveraging [persuasive technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persuasive_technology) to [discourage cognitive offloading]({{ '/projects/persuasive-strategies-mindful-ai/' | relative_url }}). The rich empirical literature in psychology, sociology, and anthropology holds vital insights for AI systems, provided we take the time to dig into those archives and bring them forward.

Alongside this, I collaborate on the [security and privacy of vehicular ad-hoc networks]({{ '/projects/vanets-security-privacy/' | relative_url }}). A full list of my work is on the [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) page, my current work is described under [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}), and the courses I have supported are listed under [teaching]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}).

Beyond my academic work, I am a passionate [photographer]({{ '/photos/' | relative_url }}) and documentary enthusiast. I am particularly drawn to history, anthropology, and sociology, with a deep fascination for how morality, ethics, and artistic expression manifest across diverse human societies.
