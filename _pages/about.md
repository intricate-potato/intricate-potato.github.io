I am Abdullah, an MCS student at [Dalhousie University](https://www.dal.ca/), where I work in the Persuasive Computing Lab with [Dr. Rita Orji](https://web.cs.dal.ca/~orji/). I am curious about what happens to people when they live alongside AI, and my research explores how language models and recommender systems shape the way we think and the way we see one another.

My work brings together [human-computer interaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human%E2%80%93computer_interaction) and [computational social science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_social_science). The social sciences hold generations of careful wisdom about people, and it excites me to carry that wisdom into the age of AI so that the technology we build can understand the people it serves a little better (sometimes it feels like a cheat-code for human-AI research to be honest.)

I love listening to people's stories, especially when they come from the people who actually lived them, since many of the most lasting lessons in my life have come from simply hearing someone out. I am also deeply grateful for every chance I have been given and for everyone who made it possible, from my mother to the elementary school teacher who once patted me on the back and blessed me to grow up to be a good person :) 

I believe the best way to do justice to these privileges and to truly express my gratitude is to give back wherever I can. This is why I mentor students and expats on things like higher education and managing money, and it still amazes me how a small piece of information can sometimes make a real difference in someone's life. I believe these small acts of kindness can grow into something far greater than any of us can imagine, and I know there are many people around the world moving forward with the same intention, whether they realize it or not.

When I step away from research, you will usually find me behind a [camera]({{ '/photos/' | relative_url }}). Through photography, and through my love of history and anthropology, I keep discovering the many ways people across the world find beauty and live by their values, and each discovery reminds me how much there is to celebrate in being human.
